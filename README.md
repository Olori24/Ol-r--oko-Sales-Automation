# Olórí-oko Sales Automation

**AI-assisted sales operations for small businesses that need more leads, faster follow-up, and less manual work.**

> Built as part of Bolaji Akande's practical AI automation portfolio.

## What it does

Olórí-oko Sales Automation is designed around the real sales workflow:

```
Lead captured
   ↓
Lead qualified
   ↓
Follow-up scheduled
   ↓
Conversation tracked
   ↓
Offer / product presented
   ↓
Payment or next action
   ↓
Customer retained
```

The goal is not to add another dashboard. The goal is to reduce the repetitive work between **finding a prospect and moving that prospect to the next meaningful sales action**.

## Portfolio focus

- Lead capture and qualification
- Automated follow-up workflows
- Sales pipeline visibility
- Customer communication
- AI-assisted sales tasks
- Repeatable workflows for small businesses
- Integration-ready automation architecture

## Why this project matters

Many small businesses run sales through WhatsApp, spreadsheets, forms and memory. That makes follow-up inconsistent and makes it difficult to know which prospects need attention.

This project explores how AI and workflow automation can turn those fragmented activities into a repeatable operating process.

## Engineering principles

- Automate repetitive work, not human judgment
- Keep customer data controlled and auditable
- Prefer deterministic workflow steps where possible
- Keep external credentials server-side
- Verify important actions before treating them as complete
- Design for low-cost deployment and practical African-market use
